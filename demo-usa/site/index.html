Gentle care for every smile in the family

A family dental practice in Springfield. We explain every treatment before it begins — and we keep same-day time for dental pain.

The practice

We are a general dentistry practice: cleanings, fillings, crowns, implants and clear aligners, for every age. When a case calls for a specialist, we refer you — and we tell you why.

Sterilization follows a tracked protocol, instrument by instrument, and X-rays are digital and taken only when they help the diagnosis.

The office is wheelchair accessible, with parking behind the building.

Les soins

Twice-a-year cleanings and complete exams are the heart of our practice. Digital X-rays are taken when they help, not by habit.

Implants are placed and restored in the office, from the first scan to the final crown. Every case starts with a written plan.

Clear aligner treatment for adults and teens, with short check-ins scheduled around school and work.

In-office whitening, after an exam of existing shades and restorations.
