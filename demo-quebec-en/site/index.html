Healthy smiles, one family at a time

A family clinic in the heart of the Plateau. New patients welcome — we take the time to explain before we treat.

The clinic

The clinic brings together four general dentists and three assistants who share a single file: yours.

Most private insurance plans are accepted: you receive a detailed receipt for your claim, and a written estimate precedes any treatment.

The clinic is wheelchair accessible, two streets from the metro, with paid parking next door.

Les soins

The complete exam and the cleaning are the base of your follow-up. X-rays are taken when they are useful, not by habit.

Implants are placed and restored at the clinic, from the first exam to the crown. Every case starts with a written plan.

Clear aligners for adults and teens, with short follow-ups planned around school and work.

Whitening happens at the clinic, after an exam of existing shades and restorations.
