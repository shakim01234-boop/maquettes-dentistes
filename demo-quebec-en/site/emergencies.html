Dental emergencies

Pain or a broken tooth cannot wait. Here is how to reach us, and what to do outside opening hours.

During opening hours

Call the front desk as soon as we open: slots are kept every day for emergencies. Describe what you feel to receive the right slot.

Outside opening hours

Call 811 (Info-Santé) to be directed. For major trauma with bleeding, go to the nearest hospital emergency room.
